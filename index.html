How it's calculated

  • Landed cost = product cost + shipping to US.
  • Customer pays = sale price + shipping charged to customer.
  • Payment fee = customer pays × fee % + fixed fee (default 2.9% + $0.30, Shopify Basic online card rate).
  • Total cost = landed cost + other costs + payment fee. Profit = customer pays − total cost.
  • Margin = profit ÷ customer pays. Markup = (customer pays − landed) ÷ landed.
  • Suggested price: the price where, after the fee and other costs, you keep landed × (1 + target markup). At 100% that's the "double your landed cost" rule with the fee covered. Rounded up to .99 as an option.
  • Break-even: the price where profit is exactly $0.
  • 50% margin badge: passes when profit is at least half of what the customer pays.

Estimates only. Doesn't include refunds, chargebacks, currency conversion (+1–1.5% for international cards), taxes or your monthly plan fee. Runs entirely in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.
